Cashew Types

4 Main Cashew Categories

Sri Lankan cashews are divided into 4 main categories based on processing level and use.

1 🌰

Raw Cashew

Gas Kaju / Poththa / Unprocessed

Shell සහ testa (poththa) සහිත cashew. Processing, roasting, export සඳහා භාවිතා කරනවා. Farmers directly sell කරන grade.

Types

🌿 Gas Kaju 📦 Bulk Cashew Nuts 🚛 Export Grade

Best For

Processing Roasting Export
Price Range
Rs. 800 – 1,000 / kg
Quality අනුව vary වෙනවා
2 🌿

Kernel / Cleaned Cashew

Suddha Kaju / Processed Kernel

Shell ඉවත් කරලා, skin remove කරලා ready to eat / pack කරන cashew. Premium quality kernels.

Sub-types

✨ Whole Kernels 🔸 Broken Kernels ⭐ Export Quality

Best For

Retail Packing Commercial Use Export
Price Range
Rs. 1,200 – 1,700 / kg
Whole kernels = higher price
3 🔥

Roasted / Flavored Cashews

Roasted / Salted / Devilled Kaju

Kernel roasting සහ flavoring කරලා ready-to-eat cashews. Consumer market සඳහා most popular grade.

Sub-types

🤍 Plain Roasted 🧂 Salted 🌶️ Devilled / Spicy 🍯 Flavored
