Our Process

From Tree to Pack —
Pure Quality

We grow, harvest, machine-cut, clean, and pack every cashew ourselves — no middlemen, no compromise on freshness.

🌳
Gas Kaju (Raw Harvest)
Fresh cashew nuts harvested directly from our trees at peak ripeness for maximum flavor and nutrition.
⚙️
Machine Processing
Industrial-grade machines cleanly split and cut each cashew kernel, preserving shape and quality.
✨
Hand Cleaning & Sorting
Every batch is hand-sorted to remove any imperfect pieces — only premium kernels make the cut.

Fresh Packing & Delivery
Hygienically packed in airtight packaging to lock in freshness, then delivered island-wide.
